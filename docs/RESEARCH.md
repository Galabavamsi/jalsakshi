# Research notes (sources for the README, video and blog)

All figures were checked on 8 Oct 2026. Re-verify before quoting on camera.

## Jal Jeevan Mission in Chhattisgarh
- **Jal Arpan order:** on 30 Sep 2026 the CG JJM Mission Director asked Collectors to hand villages with finished works and a successful 15-day trial run over to Gram Panchayats at the special Gram Sabhas of 2 Oct 2026, with IMIS entries done within a week. ([DPR CG](https://dprcg.gov.in/post/1790772080/Raipur-Jal-Arpan-Water-Offering-programs-will-be-held-on-October-2-in-villages-reported-as-Har-Ghar-Jal-Tap-Water-in-Every-Home-completed-schemes-will-be-handed-over-to-Gram-Panchayats-for-operation))
- **JJM IMIS (as on 07/10/2026):** CG has 19,658 villages, 7,603 reported Har Ghar Jal and 6,594 certified. ([report](https://ejalshakti.gov.in/JJM/JJMReports/Physical/JJMRep_HarGharJalVillage.aspx))
- **CAG audit (tabled 14 Jul 2026):** villages certified despite incomplete works; only 4 of 75 labs test all 13 parameters. ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2284560&reg=48&lang=2))
- **Korba (23 Sep 2026):** ₹516 cr paid; supply in about 110 villages under single-village schemes. ([ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/hi/state/taps-installed-without-water-source-no-water-reached-in-home-jal-jeevan-mission-sorry-state-in-korba-cts26092303858))
- **JJM 2.0** (Cabinet, 10 Mar 2026): a focus on service delivery; a Gram Panchayat certifies HGJ only once O&M is in place. Funds are reform-linked, so **do not** say it "pays per litre". ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2237548&reg=3&lang=1))
- **Jal Seva Aankalan** (launched 30 Dec 2025): self-assessment by the Village Water & Sanitation Committee, presented at the Gram Sabha and uploaded to IMIS by the Panchayat Secretary. There is no household-level feedback. ([DoWR PDF](https://www.jalshakti-dowr.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/908d7e6b2f8b17a9d61d4733f951e63d.pdf))
- **National functionality checks:** 26% of villages under mega schemes found completely non-functional. ([NDTV, Nov 2025](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/jal-jeevan-mission-finds-26-villages-without-water-centre-freezes-funds-9711803))
- **Nal Jal Mitra:** the village pump operator ("Jal Vitaran Sanchaalak", NSQF L4). The Meri Panchayat app is the official grievance channel. ([JJM 2.0 guidelines](https://jaljeevanmission.gov.in/sites/default/files/guideline/Operational-Guidelines-JJM-2.pdf))

## Groundwater and setting (Durg / Bemetara)
- **CGWB 2025 stage of extraction:** Bemetara 92.3%, Durg 76.0%. Critical blocks are Berla, Dharsiwa, Gurur, Bemetara and Nawagarh; Durg and Dhamdha are semi-critical. ([India-WRIS layer 8](https://arc.indiawris.gov.in/server/rest/services/NWIC/GWR2025_CGWB/MapServer/8/query?where=state%3D%27CG%27&outFields=block,district,class,sgw_dev_pe&returnGeometry=false&f=json))
- **Bemetara:** all 425 Gram Panchayats resolved not to grow summer paddy after handpumps dried up. ([DPR CG](https://dprcg.gov.in/post/1761743386/))
- **2026 monsoon:** CG about +6% statewide but uneven (Bemetara about 75% of normal to 19 Sep), so stress returns from Feb to May.

## Why voice, why keypad
- At the previous tour event, phone or IVR delivery appeared in about **2%** of 1,179 projects (our analysis of the public project list).
- The NextDrop (Bengaluru) water-alert pilot failed partly because utility staff reports were unreliable (70% reporting, 63% wrong) and many women had no phone of their own. So we collect from **multiple households**, support shared phones, and never trust the operator's self-report alone.
